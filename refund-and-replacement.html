Eligibility

We want you to enjoy every meal. If something is wrong with your order, you may be eligible for a refund or a replacement in these cases:

  • The order was not delivered or was significantly late.
  • Items are missing or incorrect.
  • Food arrived in poor condition, spoiled, or not prepared as described.
  • You were charged incorrectly or more than once.

Each request is reviewed individually, and we may ask for a photo of the item and your order number.

How to Request a Refund or Replacement

Please contact us as soon as possible after the issue occurs, and include your order number, name, and a short description of the problem.

Time Limits

Because our food is freshly prepared and perishable, food quality issues must be reported within 2 hours of delivery or pickup, and missing items, incorrect charges, or non-delivery within 48 hours.

Requests made after these windows may not be eligible, though we will always consider genuine circumstances.

Replacements

Where possible we will remake the affected item or order at no extra cost. Replacements are subject to kitchen hours and ingredient availability. For delivery orders, we will arrange re-delivery within our delivery area where practical.

If a replacement cannot be provided in reasonable time, we will offer a refund or store credit instead.

Non-Refundable Items

Refunds or replacements are generally not available for:

  • Orders collected or delivered as requested and consumed without any reported issue.
  • Dissatisfaction based on personal taste, where the food was prepared as described.
  • Orders delayed or missed due to an incorrect address or contact details, or no one being available to receive them.
  • Discounts, gift cards, and promotional credits.
  • Allergic reactions where the allergy was not disclosed at the time of ordering.

Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled free of charge before preparation begins. Please call us right away to cancel. Once the kitchen has started preparing your order, it cannot be cancelled or refunded, except as described under Eligibility.

Reservations

Reservations can be cancelled or changed at any time by contacting us. If a deposit was taken for a large group, the terms given at booking apply.

Payment Refunds

Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method. Once approved, refunds are usually processed within 3–5 business days, though your bank or card issuer may take additional time to show the credit.

Partial Refunds

Where only part of an order was affected, we will refund the value of the affected items only.
