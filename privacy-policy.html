Introduction

Fort Side Café & Grill ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through our website, in-store, and by phone, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Your Consent

By using our website or giving us your information, you agree to the practices described here. We handle personal information in accordance with applicable Canadian and Alberta privacy legislation.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information we need to serve you:

  • Contact details: name, email address, phone number, and delivery address.
  • Account details: login email and an encrypted password if you create an account.
  • Order and reservation details: items ordered, dates, party size, and special requests such as allergies.
  • Payment details: processed by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Technical data: device, browser, and pages visited, collected through cookies and similar tools.

How We Use Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and deliver your orders and confirm your reservations.
  • Manage your account and respond to your enquiries.
  • Send newsletters, offers, and discounts, only where you have subscribed.
  • Improve our menu, website, and customer service.
  • Meet legal, tax, and security obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing & Disclosure

We share information only where needed to run our business, such as with payment processors, delivery partners, and website hosting and email providers, who may only use it to provide their service to us.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect our rights, customers, or staff.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies to keep items in your cart, remember your preferences, and understand how the site is used. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features, such as ordering, may not work properly without them.

Security & Retention

We use reasonable physical, technical, and organisational safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, then delete or anonymise it.

Your Rights

You may ask to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and you may withdraw your consent or unsubscribe from marketing at any time using the link in our emails or by contacting us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
